    About this gallery

    Why the links live here

    Museum and archive websites change their addresses over time. Keeping the links in this gallery, rather than in the book, means they can be checked and corrected long after the book is published.

    What the labels mean

    Public domain The work is old enough to be free of copyright; free images are widely available.

    In copyright The artist or their estate still holds rights. We link to where you can view it rather than reproduce it.

    Culturally sensitive The work is sacred or restricted for the community that made it. We link to background or to the community’s own source, and explain why.

    Found a broken link?

    Links are checked regularly, but if one stops working please report it here. Every entry also offers an archived copy as a fallback.
